# 11 - Reporting / KPI / Semantic Layer

## What is KPI layer?
Business-friendly layer for reporting tools.

Examples:
- Power BI dataset
- dbt models
- views in DW

---

## Why KPI layer?
- avoids complex joins in BI
- consistent business definitions
- reduces duplication

---

## Example KPI
Daily sales:
- date
- total_sales
- total_orders
- avg_order_value

---

## Interview Tip
Always mention KPI layer as final output.
