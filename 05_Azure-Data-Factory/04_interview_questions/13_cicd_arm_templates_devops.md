# 13 - CI/CD, ARM Templates, DevOps (Interview Questions)

## 1) How do you deploy ADF pipelines to prod?
Using ARM templates generated by publish.

---

## 2) What is collaboration branch?
Branch where developers work.

---

## 3) What is publish branch?
Branch containing deployment artifacts.

---

## 4) Why parameterize linked services?
Because dev and prod have different:
- storage accounts
- SQL servers
- Key Vaults

---

## 5) How to manage triggers during deployment?
Disable triggers in prod, deploy, then enable.

---

## 6) Interview scenario
**Q:** What is the biggest CI/CD challenge in ADF?
A: linked service parameterization and secret management.

---

## 7) Best practices
- separate dev/test/prod factories
- Key Vault in prod
- automate deployment in DevOps
