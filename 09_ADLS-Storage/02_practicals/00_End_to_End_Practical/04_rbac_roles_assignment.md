# Practical 04 — RBAC Roles Assignment (IAM)

## Goal
Understand and apply RBAC roles.

## Steps
1. Storage account → Access Control (IAM)
2. Add role assignment:
   - Storage Blob Data Reader (read-only)
   - Storage Blob Data Contributor (read/write)
   - Storage Blob Data Owner (admin)

## Example
Give your Synapse managed identity:
- Storage Blob Data Contributor

## Interview point
RBAC gives access to storage resource.
