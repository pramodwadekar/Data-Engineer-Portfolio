# 05 — DP-203 Style ADLS Questions (With Answers)

1) What is HNS and why required for data lake?
**Answer:** HNS enables real directories + ACL.

2) How do you secure ADLS?
**Answer:** RBAC + ACL, managed identity, private endpoints, firewall.

3) How do you optimize cost?
**Answer:** tiers + lifecycle rules.

4) How do you monitor storage?
**Answer:** metrics + diagnostic logs to Log Analytics.

5) How do you integrate with Synapse?
**Answer:** Linked service + managed identity + RBAC + ACL.
