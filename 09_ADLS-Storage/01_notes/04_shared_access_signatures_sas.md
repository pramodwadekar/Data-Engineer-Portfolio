# 04 - SAS Tokens (Shared Access Signature)

## What is SAS?
A temporary token that grants limited access.

## Types
- Service SAS
- Account SAS
- User delegation SAS (recommended)

## Why used?
- temporary vendor access
- secure time-bound download/upload

## Why not recommended for production pipelines?
- token rotation issues
- secret management complexity
Prefer managed identity.
